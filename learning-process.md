# Learning Process

## Question 1 : What is the Feynman Technique?
The Feynman Technique means learning something by explaining it in your own simple words, as if you are teaching it to someone else.

## Question 2 : In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?
The most interesting idea for me was that we learn better when we try to explain things in our own simple words instead of just memorizing them. It made me realize that real learning comes from understanding, not remembering.

## Question 3 : What are active and diffused modes of thinking?
In active mode we fully focus on something and try our best to understand or solve it. and in diffused mode we take a break and relax, allowing your mind to think freely and come up with new ideas.

## Question 4 : According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.

1. Start with the big picture.
2. Understand the basic concepts.
3. Break the topic into smaller parts.
4. Focus on understanding, not memorizing.
5. Practice and test yourself.
6. Review what you have learned.

## Question 5 : What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

1. Focus on learning without distractions.
2. Understand concepts instead of memorizing.
3. Practice coding daily.
4. Explain concepts in my own words.
5. Manage time and complete tasks on time.
6. Stay consistent and keep learning.
