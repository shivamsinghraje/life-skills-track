## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment is any unwelcome behaviour of a sexual nature that makes a person feel uncomfortable, embarrassed or unsafe. 
It doesn't always have to be physical. Some common examples are:

- Passing comments or jokes about someone's body, looks or clothes
- Touching, hugging or standing too close to someone when they haven't agreed to it
- Asking someone out again and again, or asking for sexual favours, even after they've said no
- Sending inappropriate photos, videos or messages, including on WhatsApp, social media or office chat
- Staring at someone in an uncomfortable way or making suggestive gestures
- Spreading rumours or making remarks about someone's personal life

---

## 2. What would you do if you face or witness such behaviour?

I wouldn't ignore it or laugh it off. Even if it seems small, it can affect the person badly and it usually gets worse if nobody says anything.

**If it happens to me:**
I would first tell the person clearly and politely that this is not okay and they should stop, as long as I feel safe doing that. If it continues, or if I am not comfortable speaking to them directly, I would go to HR or the Internal Committee (IC) and report it as per the company's PoSH policy.

**If I witness it:**
I would check on the affected person, support them and make sure they know they're not alone. I definitely wouldn't join in or laugh along. I would encourage them to report it and if needed, I would report it myself.
