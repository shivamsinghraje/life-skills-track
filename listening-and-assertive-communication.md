
### Question 1. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

1. Giving our full attention to the person speaking and avoid distractions like mobile phones or other activities.
2. Do not interrupt the speaker. Allow them to complete their thoughts before responding.
3. Showing interest by using simple responses like "Yes", "Okay" or "I understand".
4. Maintaining eye contact and use positive body language, like nodding your head, to show that you are listening.
5. Asking questions whenever something is not clear instead of making assumptions.
6. Listening carefully and trying to understand the other person's thoughts rather than thinking about what to reply.
7. Paying attention to the speaker's tone, facial expressions and emotions.
8. Repeating the important points in our own words to confirming that we have understood the message correctly.

### Question 2. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

1. Give full attention to the speaker and avoid distractions.
2. Listen more and speak less so that you can understand the other person's point of view.
3. Allow some silence and give the speaker enough time to express their thoughts.
4. Try to understand the speaker's feelings and emotions, not just their words.
5. Show empathy and respect, even if you have a different opinion.
6. Pay attention to body language, facial expressions and tone of voice.
7. Repeat or summarize the important points in your own words to make sure you have understood correctly.
8. Focus on the actual topic and avoid moving to unrelated discussions.
9. Take notes during meetings and technical discussions so that important details are not missed.
10. Share the meeting notes with team members, managers or clients and ask them to verify if everything is correct.
11. Make sure everyone involved in the discussion understands and agrees on the important points.


### Question 3. What are the obstacles in your listening process?

Sometimes I get distracted by my phone, surroundings or my own thoughts while someone is speaking.
I also catch myself thinking about what to reply instead of focusing on what the person is saying.
At times, I interrupt others or make assumptions before hearing the complete conversation.
Stress, tiredness and lack of interest can also make it difficult for me to concentrate and understand everything properly.

### Question 4. What can you do to improve your listening?

I can improve my listening by giving people my full attention and avoiding distractions. 
I will try not to interrupt others and allow them to finish speaking. 
Instead of thinking about my reply, I will focus on understanding their point of view. 
I can also ask questions whenever I have doubts and repeat important points to make sure I have understood correctly. 
Practising these habits in daily conversations and office meetings will help me become a better listener.


### Question 5. When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

I sometimes become passive when talking to seniors or people with more experience because I hesitate to share my opinions. 
When I disagree with someone, I may stay quiet to avoid arguments. Sometimes I also say yes to things even when I have other work or 
feel uncomfortable saying no. I do this mainly to avoid disappointing others or creating unnecessary conflicts.

### Question 6. When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

I sometimes become aggressive when I am stressed, frustrated or overloaded with work. 
When someone repeatedly makes the same mistake or does not understand my point, I may lose patience and respond rudely. 
During arguments, I sometimes interrupt others because I want to explain my side. This usually happens when I am unable to control my 
emotions at that moment.

### Question 7. When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

Sometimes, when I am upset with someone but do not want to confront them directly, I give short replies like "fine" or "whatever". 
If I feel ignored or hurt, I may stop talking to that person instead of explaining what is bothering me. 
Sometimes I also use sarcasm to express my frustration. I realise that these reactions do not solve the problem and can create more misunderstandings.

### Question 8. How can you make your communication assertive? What are a few steps you can apply in your own life?

I can make my communication more assertive by expressing my thoughts and feelings clearly without being rude or disrespectful. 
I will try to share my opinions openly, even when talking to seniors or during team discussions. I should also learn to say no politely 
when I am not comfortable with something. Instead of reacting immediately when I am angry, I will take a moment to calm down before responding. 
I will also listen to others, respect their opinions and ask questions whenever I have doubts. By practising these habits in my daily life,
I can become more confident and communicate in a clear and respectful way.
